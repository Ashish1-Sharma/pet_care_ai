## Full description (max 4000)

Is your dog or cat sick, scratching, limping or acting strange? PetCareAI is an AI vet symptom checker that helps you understand your pet's health problem in seconds — and tells you whether it's safe to watch at home or time to see a veterinarian.

Snap a photo of the problem area, describe the symptoms, and our AI pet health scanner gives you a clear triage report: possible conditions, severity level, home care steps and emergency warning signs.

AI VET SYMPTOM CHECKER — HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose your pet: dog, cat, rabbit, bird or hamster
2. Take a photo of the skin, eye, ear, paw, wound or lump
3. Describe the symptoms in your own words
4. Get an instant AI health report with a Low / Moderate / Emergency risk level
5. Follow home care tips or find out if your pet needs a vet today
6. Re-scan later and track recovery over time

ASK THE AI VET CHAT
Have a question that isn't about a photo? Chat with our AI vet assistant about diet, behavior, vomiting, diarrhea, vaccines, medicines or anything else worrying you — any time of day or night.

WHAT EVERY SCAN INCLUDES
✔ Possible conditions with a confidence score
✔ Severity level: Low risk, Moderate or Emergency
✔ Emergency warning signs that mean "go to the vet now"
✔ Practical home care steps for mild problems
✔ Clear advice on when to see a veterinarian
✔ Before-and-after photo comparison to track healing

DOG HEALTH & CAT HEALTH PROBLEMS IT HELPS WITH
• Dog skin problems: rashes, hot spots, itching, hair loss, bald patches, dandruff
• Fleas, ticks, mites and skin infections
• Eye problems: redness, discharge, swelling, cloudy eyes
• Ear infections: bad smell, head shaking, dark wax
• Paw injuries, limping, cracked or bleeding nails
• Cuts, wounds, bites and wounds that won't heal
• Lumps, bumps, swelling and cysts
• Dull or patchy coat and excessive licking or scratching

BUILT FOR EVERY PET PARENT
New puppy or kitten owner unsure what's normal? Busy dog owner wanting a quick second opinion? Caring for an older cat with ongoing skin issues? PetCareAI gives you a fast, informed first step for your pet's health.

IMPORTANT
PetCareAI provides AI-generated information for guidance only. It is not a veterinarian and does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment. Always contact a licensed vet for serious, worsening or ongoing symptoms. In an emergency, go to your nearest animal hospital immediately.
